# pitch-idea-generator-hehe
Here’s what you can do with PitchForge:

As a client / business owner:

Drop in keywords that describe your business idea (e.g. “eco-friendly, subscription, Gen Z, wellness”) and instantly get a polished pitch you can send to investors, partners, or clients — no writing skills needed.
Use it to quickly test different angles of your idea by swapping keywords and regenerating.

As a freelancer or agency:

Use it to onboard clients faster — have them submit their keywords, then generate a first-draft proposal in seconds.
Great for sales decks, cold outreach, and RFP responses.

As a startup founder:

Generate a pitch skeleton before a meeting, then refine it manually.
Use it during brainstorming sessions to see how different keyword combinations shape a narrative.
